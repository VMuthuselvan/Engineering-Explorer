What do you love to learn, do, and solve?

Every Michigan engineer starts with a similar first year and declares a major by the third term. Tell us what you enjoy and see which of the 18 majors share those categories. This is a place to explore, not to decide.

  1. 1
    Select at least three options from each category below — Subjects I enjoy, Things I like doing, and Problems I want to work on. Subjects show what you like to learn, doing shows how you like to work, and problems show where you want your work to land. Three from each keeps one side of you from drowning out the others.
  2. 2
    Your matches stay hidden until every category has three. Once they do, matches appear and update instantly as you add or remove options. Use Reset choices to start over.
  3. 3
    Tap a match to see which of your picks drove it, the careers it leads to, and links to Michigan Engineering resources and outside career-exploration tests.

Select at least three options from each category

Three or more from Subjects I enjoy, three or more from Things I like doing, and three or more from Problems I want to work on. There are no wrong answers and nothing is scored against you. Pick what you would choose on a free afternoon, not what you think an engineer should like.
